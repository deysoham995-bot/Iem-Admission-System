About Institute of Engineering & Management (IEM)

Pioneering technical and management education in Eastern India for over 37 years.

Established in 1989, Institute of Engineering & Management (IEM) is West Bengal's oldest, most distinguished private engineering and management institution, globally celebrated for academic excellence, innovative pedagogy, and record-setting campus placements.

Holding Autonomous Status under MAKAUT and accredited with NAAC Grade 'A', IEM combines rigorous academic discipline with active industry immersion. Located strategically in Sector V, Salt Lake — Kolkata's premier IT and Electronics corridor — our students engage directly with top technology giants, startup incubators, and advanced research laboratories.
